### SLIDE 6: THEORETICAL BACKGROUND (`3:30 to 4:30`)

#### On-Slide Text
- **Section Label:** Literature Review
- **Single-Line Headline:** An **adapted TPB model** frames disposal-stage intention
- **Left Zone: TPB-Guided Campus Architecture *(Ajzen, 1991, 2020)*:**
  - Behavioral intention represents the motivational antecedent of pro-environmental action (Ayob et al., 2017; Raghu & Rodrigues, 2020), adapted here to reflect university disposal realities (Bang et al., 2025; Ling et al., 2024):
  - **Psychosocial Core:**
    - `EA` (*Navy*): Personal evaluation of resource conservation and sorting value.
    - `SN` (*Green*): Peer sorting behavior, social recognition, and campus rules.
  - **Contextual Enablers:**
    - `EF` (*Amber Gold*): Bin accessibility, clear signage, time effort, and service quality.
    - `EP` (*Medium Blue*): Sorting method know-how and exposure to campus publicity.
- **Right Zone: Scope Boundaries of `WCI`:**
  - **Point of Disposal:** Stated willingness and planned effort to place waste into designated categories before collection, distinct from downstream industrial recycling.
  - **Intention vs. Behavior:** Captures motivational readiness rather than observed behavior, preserving Ajzen's (2020) stage distinction.

#### Suggested Oral Script
> "We ground our conceptual model in a contextually adapted Theory of Planned Behavior perspective. Rather than testing core TPB in isolation, we pair internal psychosocial drivers, attitudes and social norms, with situational university enablers, namely facilities and educational publicity. We also bound waste classification intention strictly at the point of disposal, distinct from downstream recycling and observed behavior."
